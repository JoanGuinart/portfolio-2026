## The project

Typeform is one of the best-known online form builders, used by millions of people. I didn't build the product from scratch: I work on keeping it healthy and moving it forward.

## What I do

- **Improvements and updates** across the product, including the forms themselves.
- **Accessibility work** so forms can be used by everyone, including keyboard and screen reader users.
- **Maintenance** of existing features in a large React and TypeScript codebase.
