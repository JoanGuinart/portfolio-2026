## The idea

In most bars and restaurants, staff spend a big part of the shift walking from table to table just to take orders. BarPass removes that step: customers scan a QR code at their table, order from their phone, and the order goes straight to the bar. No app to install, no training for the team.

## What I built

I designed and built BarPass from scratch as a complete SaaS product:

- **Ordering flow for customers**, mobile-first, opened directly from the table's QR code.
- **Bar panel** where incoming orders appear grouped by table, so the team only has to prepare and serve.
- **Venue dashboard** to manage the whole place in one panel: menu, tables and QR codes.
- **Landing page, live demo and onboarding** to sell the product.

## Status

The product is almost finished and will be on the market soon.
