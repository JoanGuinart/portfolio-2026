## The project

The Left Bit is a Barcelona-based native software studio that builds iOS, Android, web and AI products for other companies. The company needed a new website that reflected that level of craft.

## What I did

I rebuilt the website from scratch:

- **Built with Astro** as a fast, fully static site.
- **Multilingual** in English, Spanish and Catalan.
- **Ongoing improvements** after launch, focused on SEO and GEO (visibility in AI-powered search engines) so the site keeps ranking and being cited.
